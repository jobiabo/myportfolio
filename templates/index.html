01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Engineering meets

I enjoy the full process: understanding a problem, shaping an experience, building the product and improving it through iteration.

My path brings together software development and visual design. I work across backend, web and mobile technologies, while my graphic design and video-editing experience influences how I think about clarity, presentation and user experience.
